House notes · Before you arrive

Practical answers for guests.

How far in advance should we reserve?

Dinner service books two to six weeks ahead; chef’s table requests may need longer.

Is the tasting menu fixed?

Courses follow the season. Dietary notes are taken at reservation and woven into the progression.

Do you offer wine pairings?

Yes — a cellar pairing and a juice pairing run beside the tasting spine.

Is there a dress code?

Smart and comfortable. The room is intimate; jackets are welcome, not required.
