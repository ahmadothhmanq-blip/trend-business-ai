From the editors

An essay on belonging and ambition

We began with a simple belief: serious learning deserves a setting that respects both tradition and invention. Today our community gathers scholars, practitioners, and students who treat education as a lifelong craft — measured not only in degrees conferred, but in the clarity of thought they carry into the world.

Clarifications

Readers ask

Answers from the registrar and the letters desk.

  1. 01

    How do I request a prospectus?

    Write the letters desk with your intended program. Admissions replies within two business days with term dates and requirements.

  2. 02

    Are visiting scholars welcome mid-term?

    Yes — short residencies are arranged through the dean’s office when seminar capacity allows.

  3. 03

    What languages are courses offered in?

    Primary instruction is English, with language electives and bilingual research seminars in select institutes.

  4. 04

    Can alumni audit seminars?

    Alumni fellows may audit with faculty approval. A quiet seat policy keeps the seminar table focused.
