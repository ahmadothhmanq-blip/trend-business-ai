Service

Buying desk

Practical answers for collectors and first-time clients.

How do limited editions ship?
Numbered pieces ship in atelier packaging with white-glove options in major cities within 48 hours of confirmation.
Can I reserve a fitting?
Maison and Private clients may book fittings through the contact desk. Studio pieces are ready-to-wear.
Do you restock sold-out runs?
Archive editions are not remade. Seasonal capsules may return in a new finish the following year.
What is your care policy?
Every order includes a care guide. Leather and tailoring repairs are handled by our atelier network.
